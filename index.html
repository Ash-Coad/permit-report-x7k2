[Urban Forestry - Street Trees] For each city street trees within the right-of-way, with a diameter at breast height (DBH) of 3 inches and greater, it is necessary to identify and protect the Critical Root Zone (CRZ). The CRZ is a protected circular area surrounding a tree, with a radius equal to 1.25 feet per inch of the tree’s DBH measured at 4.5 feet above grade, with the tree trunk at the center of the circle.
