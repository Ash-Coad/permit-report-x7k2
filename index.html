[Urban Forestry - Street Trees] For each city street trees within the right-of-way, with a diameter at breast height (DBH) of 3 inches and greater, it is necessary to identify and protect the Critical Root Zone (CRZ). The CRZ is a protected circular area surrounding a tree, with a radius equal to 1.25 feet per inch of the tree’s DBH measured at 4.5 feet above grade, with the tree trunk at the center of the circle. The main structural and functional portion of the root system (City Code Sec. 9-8002). If any disturbance within the critical root zone is necessary for demolition work, it is required to calculate the percentage of the CRZ of City trees that will be affected by the demolition activities. This calculation should include all activities that will cause disturbance within the CRZ. Urban Forestry will review the calculations to determine whether the trees can remain or if the disturbance is too extensive, requiring the trees to be removed and replaced. Please note that if 30% or more of the critical root zone (CRZ) of a City tree is disturbed, the tree will need to be removed and replaced. Please label any trees as "to be removed" on the site/plot plan survey if the proposed work will disturb too much of the trees CRZ. PRCR - Urban Forestry (Turner Robert) · *Approved · 2026-07-09
[Urban Forestry - Street Trees] For each city street trees within the right-of-way, with a diameter at breast height (DBH) of 3 inches and greater, it is necessary to identify and protect the Critical Root Zone (CRZ). The CRZ is a protected circular area surrounding a tree, with a radius equal to 1.25 feet per inch of the tree’s DBH measured at 4.5 feet above grade, with the tree trunk at the center of the circle. The main structural and functional portion of the root system (City Code Sec. 9-8002). If any disturbance within the critical root zone is necessary for demolition work, it is required to calculate the percentage of the CRZ of City trees that will be affected by the demolition activities. This calculation should include all activities that will cause disturbance within the CRZ. Urban Forestry will review the calculations to determine whether the trees can remain or if the disturbance is too extensive, requiring the trees to be removed and replaced. Please note that if 30% or more of the critical root zone (CRZ) of a City tree is disturbed, the tree will need to be removed and replaced. Please label any trees as "to be removed" on the site/plot plan survey if the proposed work will disturb too much of the trees CRZ. Can the Limits of Disturbance be adjusted to not encroach into the critical root zone? [resolved]
